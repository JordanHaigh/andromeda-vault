# Job Sequencing

## What it does

For unit-time jobs with deadlines and profits, sort by descending profit and place each job in the latest available slot no later than its deadline. A simple slot scan is O(nD); disjoint-set predecessor tracking improves scheduling. Different duration models require a different formulation.
