# Minimum S T Cut

## What it does

A cut partitions vertices into S and T with source in S and sink in T; its capacity sums edges from S to T. The max-flow min-cut theorem equates minimum cut capacity with maximum flow. Residual reachability after a maximum flow identifies a minimum cut.
