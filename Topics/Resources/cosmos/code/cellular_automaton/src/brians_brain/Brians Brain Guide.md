# Brians Brain

## What it does

A three-state cellular automaton with states ready, firing, and refractory. A ready cell fires when exactly two neighbors are firing; firing becomes refractory, and refractory returns to ready. The visual patterns depend strongly on initial conditions and boundary handling.
