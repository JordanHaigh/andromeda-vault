# Coverage Gaps

The vault now has working guides and connected examples for business outcomes, organizational technical leadership, agentic delivery, end-to-end engineering, and people leadership. The remaining gaps depend on evidence from real projects and conversations; adding invented results would weaken the notes rather than improve them.

## Open items requiring real-world evidence

- **Measure released work:** For a suitable production change, record the original baseline, adoption, user or operational outcomes, cost, guardrails, and review findings. Connect the evidence to [[Topics/Business Analysis/Benefits Realisation and Outcome Measurement]].
- **Evaluate an agentic workflow:** Run a bounded, low-risk pilot using [[Topics/Business Analysis/Agentic Delivery Pilot and Evaluation]]. Record a human comparison or baseline, task quality, defects, policy failures, repair effort, cost, and post-release outcome before deciding whether to expand.
- **Validate leadership scope:** Compare current examples with the actual role expectations in the user's organization and request feedback from peers, stakeholders, and a manager. Keep personal contribution, team contribution, and measured results distinct in [[Topics/Business Analysis/Leadership Evidence and Development]].
- **Add verified project detail:** Where project records are available, turn the illustrative [[Topics/Case Studies/Reliable Data Import]] and [[Topics/Case Studies/Versioned API and Online Migration]] into factual, anonymized case studies. Do not imply either scenario describes a shipped system unless the evidence supports that claim.
