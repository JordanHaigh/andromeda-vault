## Senior skills and technical leadership

The broader practice guides are linked from [[Topics/Senior Engineering/Index]], [[Topics/Technical Leadership/Index]], and the curated [[Topics/External Reading/Engineering Leadership Resources]].

A senior engineer creates value by connecting business needs to good technical decisions, helping a team deliver, and ensuring changes work for users after release. The CV provides evidence to build on:

- Leading a small team of senior developers, setting technical direction, planning delivery, decomposing work, and reviewing pull requests.
- Designing a serverless .NET/AWS foundation using event messaging, event sourcing, and CQRS, while restructuring work so engineers could deliver in parallel.
- Working with stakeholders through requirements workshops, design approvals, and demonstrations.
- Mentoring junior developers, documenting technical decisions, and creating onboarding and release-testing guidance.
- Improving logging and automated testing while investigating real processing failures and data-quality edge cases.
- Building production data-processing and geospatial tools for rail customers across multiple countries.

The recent cloud transformation program ended before production release, so the CV does not claim measured production outcomes for that system. Keep design and delivery achievements distinct from benefits that would only be verified after release.

Start with [[Topics/Business Analysis/Leadership Evidence and Development]] for a personal strengths map and practical development goals. Related guides: [[Topics/Architecture and Senior Engineering]] and [[Topics/Business Analysis/Technical Leadership in Agentic Engineering]].

## Business analysis and problem framing

Develop the ability to move from a requested feature to a tested business need: map users and stakeholders, understand the current process and exceptions, separate assumptions from evidence, compare non-software and software options, describe a measurable future state, and trace important requirements to acceptance and outcome measures. See [[Topics/Business Analysis/Business Analysis and Solution Discovery]].

## Agentic programming and forward-looking engineering

As agents take on more implementation, engineering effort shifts toward specifying intent, supplying business and codebase context, designing bounded work, setting permissions, building evaluation and feedback loops, and reviewing outcomes. Programming knowledge remains important for architecture, security, debugging, code review, and validating agent work. The opportunity is to apply that knowledge at a higher level: identify valuable problems and shape reliable software solutions with agents.

Study [[Topics/Business Analysis/Technical Leadership in Agentic Engineering]] for task briefs, harness design, risk boundaries, human review, and outcome validation.
